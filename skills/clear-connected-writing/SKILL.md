# Clear Connected Writing

Help the reader follow a line of thought without having to reconstruct the links between isolated statements. Improve the reasoning expressed by the prose while preserving the writer's intent, evidence, qualifications and voice. Paragraph shape should follow its purpose; do not impose a recurring formula.

This skill provides language and revision guidance. It does not authorise new research, sending, publishing, changing a live document or altering its visual design. Use the current task's authority and the relevant document tools for those actions. A request for a critique remains a critique unless revision is also requested.

## Establish the writing task

Use the supplied material to identify the reader, purpose, medium, level of technical knowledge and desired degree of intervention. Infer these where the context is clear. Ask only when the missing information would materially change the result. A small edit should not begin with a questionnaire.

For a revision, distinguish three scopes:

- **Copy edit:** improve wording and local connections while retaining order and meaning.
- **Selective flow revision:** improve weak passages, paragraph links and explanations; leave effective passages alone.
- **Structural revision:** reconsider sequence, grouping and emphasis when the user requests it or accepts the proposed change.

Follow explicit user and document requirements. Otherwise, use a direct, measured voice, Australian/British spelling and plain ASCII punctuation in new prose. Avoid em dashes and smart quotes. Preserve exact quotations, names, citations, code, symbols and required identifiers where changing them would damage accuracy. Do not impose these defaults on a document that already has a clear, different house style.

Choose context from the subject of the request, not the account, folder or previous task. Do not import facts, examples, terminology or voice samples from an unrelated personal, academic or organisational context. Read only the source material relevant and authorised for this task. The reusable method contains no identity profile.

## Develop the line of thought

For a substantial passage, identify what the reader already understands, what the passage adds and why that addition matters to the current purpose. Make the connection visible in the prose. It may be a mechanism, comparison, consequence, qualification, example or unresolved question.

A paragraph normally has a coherent centre, but it need not contain a claim, evidence, limitation and implication in a fixed order. An explanation or comparison can develop across adjacent paragraphs. Give those paragraphs distinct work to do and make their relationship clear. Keep a short opening when it defines a term, states a decisive result or provides useful emphasis. Combine it with the next sentence when it merely announces the explanation that follows.

For a long draft, read [prose-and-continuity.md](references/prose-and-continuity.md) and use a brief reverse outline before polishing individual sentences. For a short email or a local edit, work directly at the relevant scale.

## Make the evidence useful

Explain what a result means, what conditions produced it and why it bears on the reader's question. Do not turn every passage into a paper summary or append a generic relevance sentence to every paragraph. Relevance can be established at the section level and developed through specific comparisons.

Preserve facts, numbers, units, denominators, dates, citation placement, assumptions and degrees of certainty. Keep observations, reported findings, interpretations, proposals and scenarios distinguishable. A style edit must not turn a modelled result into an observed one, association into causation, or a possible outcome into a commitment.

If the sources do not establish the missing connection, flag it rather than inventing it. Changing a reference or adding a factual claim is a substantive change, not an invisible style repair. Consult [audience-and-evidence.md](references/audience-and-evidence.md) for academic synthesis, technical explanation, decisions, correspondence and visual explanations.

## Revise with restraint

Fix problems in the order that matters: meaning and argument, sequence and connections, paragraph development, then sentences and wording. Use only the passes the task needs. Preserve effective short sentences, precise definitions, equations, captions and deliberate parallel structure. Avoid replacing choppy prose with overloaded sentences or adding variation merely to look less regular.

Prefer concrete subjects, verbs and reasons. Remove inflated claims, empty signposting and repeated explanations when they add no meaning. Keep uncertainty that changes how a claim should be understood. Do not replace the writer's recognisable voice with a generic professional register, especially in personal messages.

Use [revision-and-feedback.md](references/revision-and-feedback.md) for whole-document review, tracked changes or collaboration with the writer. Use [examples.md](references/examples.md) to calibrate choices, not as a phrase bank. For the public guidance behind selected principles, see [sources.md](references/sources.md).

## Check and deliver

Read the revised passage with the preceding and following text where available. Check that each transition expresses the intended relationship, that the reader can locate qualifications and references, and that the explanation answers the actual question. Compare the revision with the source for changes in meaning as well as wording.

Deliver the requested draft or critique. For substantial revisions, briefly identify consequential changes and unresolved evidence questions; do not narrate every edit. If the user asks to review changes, provide a reviewable comparison or use authorised tracked changes without deleting existing comments or accepting others' revisions. Verify any authorised saved or live output through the relevant tool before describing it as updated.

The objective is clear, credible writing that the writer can stand behind. Do not promise to make text undetectable as AI-assisted, introduce artificial errors or treat detector scores as a quality measure. Follow applicable disclosure and authorship requirements without adding unrequested attribution to ordinary drafts.
